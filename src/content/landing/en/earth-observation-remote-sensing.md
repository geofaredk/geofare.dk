faq:
  title: Questions we get asked
  items:
    - question: Which satellite data do you use?
      answer: Mostly the free and open missions of the European Copernicus programme, Sentinel-1 (radar) and Sentinel-2 (optical), plus Landsat. Where a question needs finer detail or a specific date, we add commercial imagery and tell you what it costs before we order it.
    - question: How quickly can you deliver a flood map?
      answer: With a prepared baseline, a first extent map within a day of the satellite passing over; without one, within a few days. Validated maps with depth and duration take longer, usually one to two weeks.
    - question: Do we need a GIS team to use the results?
      answer: No. You get what fits your team. A report with figures, GIS layers that open in QGIS or ArcGIS, or a web map that opens in a browser. If you have a GIS team, we deliver so they can carry on from there.
    - question: What does it cost?
      answer: A one-off analysis starts at a few days of work; ongoing monitoring is priced per area and interval. After the first conversation you get a fixed quote, and we say so when an open-data route answers the question just as well.
    - question: Can you work with imagery we already have?
      answer: Yes. Send us what you have or give us access to your subscription, and we process it alongside the open data.
    - question: How accurate are satellite-based maps?
      answer: It depends on the sensor, the resolution and the ground. We say what the uncertainty is for your case, validate against ground truth where we can, and never present a pixel as a fact it is not.
